# GoStone learning curriculum

The course follows a practical progression: first learn the rules, then learn
why groups live or die, and only then add tactical reading and whole-board
judgement. Every lesson should contain one short idea and one board action.

## Chapter 1 — The basic rules

Status: implemented.

1. Place a stone
2. Liberties
3. Capture
4. Escape from atari
5. Connect stones
6. Surround territory

## Chapter 2 — Life and death

Status: implemented.

1. One eye — useful, but not unconditionally alive
2. Two true eyes — the basic independent life shape
3. False eyes — an apparent eye can disappear when a surrounding stone is captured
4. The vital point — the key point inside an eye space
5. Make life — play the vital point yourself to create two eyes
6. Seki — mutual life can exist without either group having two eyes

The chapter deliberately teaches false eyes and seki together with the
two-eyes rule. This prevents the common but incorrect simplification that every
living group must always have two eyes.

## Chapter 3 — Capture and fight

Status: implemented.

1. Atari direction — force the opponent toward the edge
2. Double atari — threaten two groups with one move
3. Edge capture — the edge removes escape liberties
4. Ladder — repeated ataris, with a warning about ladder breakers
5. Ko — immediate recapture is forbidden
6. Capturing race — count outside liberties before playing

## Chapter 4 — Shape and connection

Planned.

1. Solid connection and cutting points
2. Diagonal connection
3. Tiger's mouth
4. Bamboo joint
5. Net
6. Snapback

## Chapter 5 — Playing the whole board

Planned.

1. Corners, sides, then centre
2. Territory and influence
3. Extension from a wall
4. Approach a corner
5. Reduce or invade
6. Play the biggest area

## Chapter 6 — Finish and score (Japanese rules)

Planned.

1. Recognise the end of the game
2. Dame and valuable endgame moves
3. Pass when no valuable move remains
4. Agree on dead stones
5. Count territory and prisoners
6. Komi and the final result

## Content rules

- One idea and one action per screen; explanations stay below three short sentences.
- The player to move and the goal must always be explicit.
- Every target move must be legal and every teaching claim must be covered by a
  deterministic position test.
- “Two eyes live” is taught with the necessary distinctions: true eyes, false
  eyes, capturable surrounding stones, and seki.
- Ladder lessons must mention ladder breakers; ko lessons must distinguish the
  immediate recapture from a later legal recapture.
- Scoring lessons use Japanese territory scoring and player agreement about dead
  stones, consistent with GoStone's game rulebook.
